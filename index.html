Nonny, My Elysium A Quiet Kind of Wonder Nonny walks in quiet light A soft eclipse along the street The kind that turns a passer's eyes As though dawn brushed past their feet. Her grace is a whispered rumour A bloom unfolding out of time I watch pretending not to watch Holding wonder like a rhyme If day must pass before we speak Let this feeling simply stay A seed tucked within my chest Gathering courage day by day Some feelings are too beautiful to rush. ♡
